FAQ

Product

What is Future Trace?
AI career intelligence: see how AI may affect your role, get next-role ideas, and track a career goal.
Do I need an account?
No. You can use Career Scan without signing up.
Is it free?
Yes for the current MVP features (scan, next roles, radar, local tracking).
What does “Find my next roles” do?
Suggests realistic adjacent roles based on your current role and context, with rough salary/timeline guidance.
What is AI Disruption Radar?
A view of how AI may impact your current role vs. recommended next roles.

Privacy & data

Where is my scan data stored?
On your device. Career scans are not uploaded to our servers.
What do you collect if I join Early Access?
Email, current role, and next role of interest — only to notify you about updates.
Can I delete my data?
Yes: use Delete Local Data in Settings, or uninstall the app. Email support@futuretrace.ai to remove a waitlist email.
Do you sell my data?
No.

Early Access / waitlist

What is Early Access?
Optional waitlist for future features (e.g. deeper transition plans). No charge to join.
I joined but didn’t get a confirmation email
Joining saves you on the list; we may not send an immediate email. You’re still registered if signup succeeded.
How do I leave the waitlist?
Email support@futuretrace.ai with the address you used.

App usage

Why don’t I see results for my job title?
Some titles need clarification; try a closer technology/role match or use “Other” where offered.
Can I track more than one career goal?
One active goal at a time; tracking another replaces the current one.
Does Contact Support work?
It opens email to support@futuretrace.ai. Install/configure Mail (or another email app) on your device.
