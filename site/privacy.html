Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 18, 2026

Welcome to Future Trace ("the App"). Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices regarding your information.

1. Information We Collect

Career Information

When you use the App, you may enter:

  • Your current job title
  • Optional career context you choose to provide

This information is used solely to generate your personalized career insights within the App.

Waitlist Information

If you choose to join our waitlist, we collect:

  • Email address

Your email is used only to notify you about future features, product updates, and early access opportunities.

Local App Data

The App stores certain information locally on your device, including:

  • Previous career scans
  • Saved career goals
  • App preferences

This information remains on your device unless you delete the App or clear the app's data.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

At this time, we do not:

  • Create user accounts
  • Collect passwords
  • Sell your personal information
  • Share your career data with advertisers
  • Track your browsing activity outside the App

3. How We Use Your Information

We use information to:

  • Generate career intelligence insights
  • Display AI disruption assessments
  • Recommend adjacent career paths
  • Save your scans locally
  • Notify waitlist subscribers about new features (only if you joined the waitlist)
  • Improve the App and fix issues

4. Data Storage

Career scans and saved career goals are stored locally on your device.

Waitlist email addresses are stored securely in our backend solely for product communication.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information.

We may use trusted third-party service providers to operate the App, such as hosting, analytics, or email delivery services. These providers process information only as necessary to provide their services.

6. AI-Generated Information

Career recommendations, AI disruption scores, salary estimates, transition timelines, and related insights are generated using artificial intelligence and publicly available career information.

These results are intended for informational purposes only and should not be considered professional career, financial, or legal advice.

7. Your Choices

You may:

  • Delete your locally stored career history by uninstalling the App or clearing app data (or using in-app deletion features, if available).
  • Choose whether or not to join the waitlist.
  • Contact us to request removal of your waitlist email.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for individuals under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the Effective Date above.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or your information, please contact us at:

Email: support@futuretrace.ai
